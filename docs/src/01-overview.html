Account & Credential Sharing — Identity Visibility for the SOC

Correlates Microsoft Defender for Identity (on-premises Active Directory), Microsoft Entra ID sign-in, risk and audit data, Defender for Endpoint logon telemetry and UEBA into one view of who is sharing accounts, passwords and sessions — plus the red-team tradecraft used to obtain and reuse other people's credentials.
Account sharing is never proven by telemetry — it is inferred. Every panel produces an indicator; the Overview score is a transparent, weighted roll-up of those indicators. Treat a high score as a triage queue, not a verdict.
